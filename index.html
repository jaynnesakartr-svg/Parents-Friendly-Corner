Tap a question to see the answer. 😊

👧👦 How many children are in PRE KG1D?

As of 31 August 2026, PRE KG1D has 22 students in total: 10 girls and 12 boys.

🏃 When is PE, and will there be a class timetable?

The class timetable is still being finalised. Once it is ready, it will be shared with parents.

The timetable will cover the children’s daily lessons and activities, including phonics, literacy, maths, Arabic, PE, library visits and more. PE day and time are also being finalised. On PE days, children will come to school in their PE kit and trainers. 😊
👕👟 What is a PE kit, and what are trainers?

A PE kit means the sports/PE clothes children wear for their PE lessons, together with a pair of trainers (sports shoes).

For now, the normal school uniform is fine, along with a pair of trainers. For PE, children can wear shorts or black leggings with sports shoes. 😊
